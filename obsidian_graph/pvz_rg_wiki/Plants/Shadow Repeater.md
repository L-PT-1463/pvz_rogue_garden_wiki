## Origins

Shadow Repeater is a new Rogue Garden universe plant, created for PvZ Lawnmaggedon, as an elemental counterpart of the [[Repeater]], allowing the [[Shadow Peashooter]] mutation to be more viable.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Shadow Repeater is a potted [[Peashooter]] mutation shooter plant.

It shoots two peas at a time in a straight line at the zombie closest to the player, that slow down the hit zombie.

If the player has at least on [[Moonflower]], Shadow Repeater shoots two piercing lasers instead. These also slow hit zombies down.

If another Peashooter-type plant is currently targeting the closest zombie, Shadow Repeater attacks the second closest instead, and so on.
### Mutations

Shadow Repeater is a mutation of the [[Peashooter]], obtained by fusing a Peashooter and a [[Shadow Peashooter]], by applying [[Fertilizer]] to the latter, or by splicing a [[Repeater]] and a [[Moonbean]].
