## Origins

Laser Bean is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2, where it fires weak lasers at the zombies that can pierce through the entire lane.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Laser Bean is a regular plant from the [[Far Future]] that fires a laser down its lane, as long as there is a zombie there.

<font color="#4bacc6">Trait</font>: Its attack has both the *Strike-through* traits. As such, it can hit multiple zombies but the attack is blocked by shields.
