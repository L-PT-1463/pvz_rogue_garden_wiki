## Origins

Cardboard Peashooter is an official Plants vs Zombie plant from the I, Zombie mini-game in the first Plants vs Zombies game.
## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Cardboard Peashooter is a regular plant from the [[Training Grounds]].

It shoots a *[[Flammable]]* pea down its lane, making a Straight attack if there is a zombie in range.
