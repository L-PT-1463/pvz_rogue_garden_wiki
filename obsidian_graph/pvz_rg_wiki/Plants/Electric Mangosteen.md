## Origins

Electric Mangosteen is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2 China, where it attacks zombies with electricity in a 3x3 area and grows in stages over time.

Officially, it is known as Mangosteen (or Charging Mangosteen, literal translation). Its name was changed for clarity.
