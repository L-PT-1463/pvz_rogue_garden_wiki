## Origins

Primal Violet is based on the Shrinking Violet, an official plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2. More specifically, Primal Violet takes after the Shrinking Violet's visual design in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where it has sharp teeth and spikes on its head.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Shrinking Violet is a regular plant from the [[Jurassic Marsh]]. It shrinks zombies in an area around itself before vanquishing, and bites zombies in the tile in front of it.

<font color="#8064a2">When vanquished</font>: Gives all zombies in a 3x3 area centred on itself the *[[Tiny]]* trait.
