## Origins

Corn Dog is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where, when a zombie is placed, Corn Dog moves to that lane.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Corn Dog is a regular plant from the [[Neon Carnival]] and the [[Jurassic Marsh]], where it is exclusive to [[Hunting Grounds]] gardens.

It bites at any tile in the lane directly in front of itself, moving to a different lane to do so. It makes a Straight attack if there is a zombie in range.
