## Origins

Hibernating Beary is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where it is a no-damage plant that gains damage when attacked.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Hibernating Beary is a protector plant from the [[Jurassic Marsh]].

It scratches at the tile directly in front of itself, making a Straight attack if there is a zombie in range, which increases in damage each time Hibernating Beary is attacked. This attack is only obtained after Hibernating Beary has been attacked for the first time.
