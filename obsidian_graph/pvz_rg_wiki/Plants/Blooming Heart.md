## Origins

Blooming Heart is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2, where it attacks zombies, imposing a stat that increases the damage that zombie takes from all Blooming Hearts.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Blooming Heart is a regular plant from the [[Neon Carnival]].

It makes a Lobbed attack at the front-most zombie in its lane, firing a heart bud. Each time it damages a zombie, Blooming Heart's damage is increased.
