## Origins

Marine Bean is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where it gains a stat boost based on the number of amphibious plants on the garden.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Marine Bean is a regular *[[Amphibious]]* plant from the [[Pirate's Beach]].

It spits water down its lane, making a Straight attack if there is a zombie in range.

Marine Bean's toughness and attack damage are boosted proportionally to the number of *[[Amphibious]]* or *[[Aquatic]]* plants in the garden.
