## Origins

Cardboard Repeater  is an official Plants vs Zombie plant from the I, Zombie mini-game in the first Plants vs Zombies game.
## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Cardboard Repeater is a regular plant from the [[Training Grounds]] that attacks in a straight line, shooting if there is a zombie in range. It has the *Repeat* trait, so fires twice per turn.

<font color="#92d050">Speciality</font>: Its attacks have the *Flammable* trait.
