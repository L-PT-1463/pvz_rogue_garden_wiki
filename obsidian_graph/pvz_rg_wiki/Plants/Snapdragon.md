## Origins

Snapdragon is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2, where it spits fire in the 2x3 area in front of itself.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Snapdragon is a regular plant from the [[Pirate's Beach]] that attacks by breathing fire onto the zombies.

<font color="#9bbb59">Speciality</font>: Its attack deals damage in its row and the row in front, on all three lanes.

<font color="#4bacc6">Trait</font>: It has the *[[Pierce]]* trait, meaning its fire can hit zombies hiding behind a shield.
<font color="#4bacc6">Trait</font>: Its attacks have the *[[Fire]]* trait.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Snapdragon is a potted [[Chomper]] mutation melee plant.

It spits fire in an area of effect that can hit multiple zombies, targeting in its centre the zombie closest to it.
### Mutations

Snapdragon is a mutation of the [[Chomper]], obtained by splicing a [[Cherry Bomb]] and a [[Chomper]].
