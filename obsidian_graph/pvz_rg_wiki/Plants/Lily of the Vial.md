## Origins

Lily of the Vial is an official Plants vs Zombies plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2 China, where it lobs flower buds that poison zombies on impact. Its poison can stack.

Officially, it is called Convallaria Pharmacist (or Convallaria Chemist, literal translation) after the scientific name of the plant species it is based on. In the Rogue Garden universe, its name is a portmanteau of Lily of the Valley, the common name of the plant species it is based on, and vial.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Lily of the Vial is a regular plant from the [[Steam City]] that throws vials down its lane.

<font color="#4bacc6">Trait</font>: Its attacks inflict the zombie with 2 stacks of *[[Poison]]*. The poison deals damage equal to the number of stacks at the start of the turn before reducing by one.
