## Origins

Primal Potato Mine is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2, where it serves as [[Potato Mine]]'s primal counterpart, exploding in a wider radius.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Primal Potato Mine is a pass-through plant that deals heavy damage on its own tile and surrounding tiles, but vanquishes itself after attacking.

<font color="#4bacc6">Trait</font>: It has the *[[Tiny]]* trait, and can therefore avoid ranged attacks.

<font color="#c0504d">Weakness</font>: It must arm itself before it can attack, making it vulnerable on the first rounds.
