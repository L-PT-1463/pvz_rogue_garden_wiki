# Puff-shroom

Puff-shroom is a mushroom that fires a cloud of spores, hitting all zombies in a medium range in front of it.

## Origins

Puff-shroom is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it can be planted for free and attacks in a straight line, but only on a short range and falls asleep during the day.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Puff-shroom is a regular *[[Tiny]]* plant from the [[Dark Ages]], exclusive to [[Mushroom Grotto]] gardens.

It shoots spores down its lane at a limited range, making a Straight attack if there is a zombie in range.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Puff-shroom is a base potted shooter plant.

It fires spores in a straight line at the zombie closest to the player, but has a limited attack range.

If another Puff-shroom-type plant is currently targeting the closest zombie, Puff-shroom attacks the second closest instead, and so on.
