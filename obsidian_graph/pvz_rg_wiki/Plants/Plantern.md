## Origins

Plantern is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it serves as a support plant that clears up fog in an area of effect.

Its weak attack in PvZ ZPG is based on Plantern's attack from its appearance in Plants vs Zombies Heroes.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Plantern is a regular plant from the [[Neighbour's House]].

It creates strong flashes of light, making a Straight *[[Stun]]* attack at the tile directly in front of it if there is a zombie there.

Plantern generates light in a 3x3 area. This clears [[Fog]], and any zombie entering or starting its turn in the light loses 1 stack of the *[[Invisible]]* trait, if the zombie has it.
