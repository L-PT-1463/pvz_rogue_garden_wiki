## Origins

Sun-shroom is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it serves as the nighttime sun producer, being cheaper than [[Sunflower]] but worse at first.

___

Sunnier-shroom is also an official plant, making its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes. It is the evolution of Sun-shroom, behaving no differently than Sun-shroom's final stage in the mainline games. It is used for PvZ ZPG, where like in Heroes, the final stage behaves as its own plant.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Sun-shroom is a regular plant from the [[Dark Ages]], exclusive to [[Mushroom Grotto]] gardens. 

<font color="#9bbb59">Speciality</font>: At the start of the third turn, it turns into a Sunnier-shroom.

<font color="#8064a2">When hit by a Munch-type attack</font>: It heals the zombie that bit it

<font color="#4bacc6">Trait</font>: It has the *[[Tiny]]* trait, and can therefore avoid ranged attacks. After one turn, it grows larger and loses its *Tiny* trait.
