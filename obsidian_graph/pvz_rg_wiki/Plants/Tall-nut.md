## Origins

Tall-nut is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it serves as a strong wall plant that counters the [[Pole Vaulting Zombie]] and [[Dolphin Rider Zombie]].

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Tall-nut is a spawned [[Wall-nut]] mutation protector plant.

When used as part of Nate's ability, a Tall-nut is occasionally planted at random spots of the lawn, where it stays, blocking the path of zombies until it is vanquished.
### Mutations

Tall-nut is a mutation of the [[Wall-nut]], obtained by fusing a Wall-nut and a [[Jugger-nut]], or by applying [[Fertilizer]] to the latter.
