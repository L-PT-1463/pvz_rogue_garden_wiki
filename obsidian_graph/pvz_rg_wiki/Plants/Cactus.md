## Origins

Cactus is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it serves as a counter to the flying [[Balloon Zombie]], but otherwise acts like a [[Peashooter]].

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Cactus is a regular plant that attacks in a straight line, shooting a single thorn if there is a zombie in range. It has the *Pierce* trait, meaning its thorn can hit zombies hiding behind a shield.
