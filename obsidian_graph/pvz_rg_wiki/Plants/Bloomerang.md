# Bloomerang

Bloomerang is a straight-shooter plant that fires boomerangs at the zombies, returning to the Bloomerang to hit the zombies a second time.

## Origins

Bloomerang is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2, where it acts fires boomerangs that can hit up to three zombies or obstacles before returning to the Bloomerang, dealing damage a second time.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Bloomerang is a regular plant from [[Old Wild Egypt]].

It fires Straight *[[Pierce]]* and *[[Strike-through]]* attacks if there are zombies in range, firing boomerangs that return to Bloomerang after traversing five tiles or three zombies, whichever comes first, as long as the boomerang is not destroyed by a [[Shield (Type)]] armour, hitting zombies a second time.
