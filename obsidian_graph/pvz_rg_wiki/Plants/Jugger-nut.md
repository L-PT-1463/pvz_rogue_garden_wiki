## Origins

Jugger-nut is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Graden Warfare as a power under the Iron Maiden name. It was later added to Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where it is called Jugger-nut, and features the Armoured trait to reduce damage taken.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Jugger-nut is protector plant from [[The Zombosseum]].

It does not attack. If granted an attack, it makes a Straight attack at a zombie in the tile directly in front of itself.

Jugger-nut has the *[[Armoured]] (1)* trait.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Jugger-nut is a spawned protector plant, and a Level 1 [[Wall-nut]] mutation.

A Jugger-nut appears on a random spot of the lawn, where it stays, blocking the path of zombies until it is vanquished.
### Mutations

Jugger-nut is a mutation of the [[Wall-nut]], obtained by fusing two Wall-nuts.
