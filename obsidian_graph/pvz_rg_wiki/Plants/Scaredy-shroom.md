# Scaredy-shroom

Scaredy-shroom is a straight-shooter mushroom that attacks zombies from afar, but gets scared and hides if the zombies get too close.

## Origins

Scaredy-shroom is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it is a cheap night-time counterpart to the [[Peashooter]], with the downside of cowering when zombies are near.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Scaredy-shroom is a regular plant from the [[Dark Ages]], exclusive to [[Mushroom Grotto]] gardens. It attacks in a straight line, shooting a single spore if there is a zombie in range.

<font color="#9bbb59">Speciality</font>: If there are zombies in a 3x3 area around it, the Scaredy-shroom loses the ability to attack but becomes a protector plant instead.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Scaredy-shroom is a potted [[Puff-shroom]] mutation shooter plant.

It fires a cloud of spores in a straight line at the zombie closest to the player, but can't attack if there are zombies near the Scaredy-shroom.

If another Puff-shroom-type plant is currently targeting the closest zombie, Scaredy-shroom attacks the second closest instead, and so on.
### Mutations

Scaredy-shroom is a mutation of the [[Puff-shroom]], obtained by fusing two Puff-shrooms.
