Squash is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it's a single use plant that squishes a zombie or group of zombies in a short range.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Winter Squash is a regular plant from the [[Neighbour's House]], where it is exclusive to [[Winter]] gardens, and the [[Jurassic Marsh]], where it is exclusive to [[Frostbite Cave]] gardens.

It launches itself at the zombies, making a Lobbed attack at the tile directly in front or behind itself if there is a zombie in range, and vanquishing itself as it does so.

When any zombie is affected by a *[[Chill]]* or *[[Freeze]]* attack, Winter Squash instantly makes a Straight *[[Pierce]]* attack targeted at that zombie.
