Health-nut is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where it is a plant with damage, which instead attacks using its health value.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Health-nut is a protector plant from the [[Training Grounds]].

It hits zombies in the tile directly in front of itself, making a Straight attack if there is a zombie in range, which deals damage proportionally to how much health Health-nut has left.
