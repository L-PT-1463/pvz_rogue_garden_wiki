## Origins

Cosmic Pea is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where it is a pea plant that conjures a Pea, granting it double-strike.

It makes a return in Plants vs Zombies 2 China, where it upgrades into a Cosmic Repeater and Gatling Pea after receiving [[Plant Food]].

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Cosmic Pea is a regular plant from [[ZCorp IST Headquarters]].

It shoots cosmic peas down its lane, making a *[[Double-Strike]]* Straight attack if there is a zombie in range.

While in [[ZCorp IST Headquarters]], if a [[Time Twist]] event takes place, Cosmic Pea turns into a random [[Pea Tribe]] plant. That plant's attacks gain *[[Double-Strike]]*.
