## Origins

Holly Pawn is based on the Holly Barrier, an official plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2. More specifically, Holly Pawn takes after the Holly Barrier's projectiles, which act as weak walls once planted.

The Holly Pawn's name is in reference to the Holly Barrier's code name Holly Knight, via the association with the pawn and knight chess pieces.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Holly Pawn is a standalone spawned protector plant.

When picked from plant selection, a Holly Pawn is launched onto a random spot of the lawn. When it lands, it damages all zombies in a small area of effect, knocking them back.

The Holly Pawn then stays there until vanquished, dealing damage to zombies in a small area of effect.

It can also be created by the [[Holly Knight]].
