## Origins

Mirror-nut is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where it attacks the zombie hero whenever any nut is damaged.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Mirror-nut is a protector plant from the [[Neon Carnival]].

It does not attack.

When any [[Nut Tribe]], including itself, is hit by an attack, Mirror-Nut makes a Straight attack at the zombie which damaged it.
