## Origins

Cardboard Umbrella Leaf is an official Plants vs Zombie plant from the I, Zombie mini-game in the first Plants vs Zombies game

Its attack in PvZ ZPG is taken directly from [[Umbrella Leaf]]'s attack animation in Plants vs Zombies Heroes.
## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Cardboard Umbrella Leaf is a regular plant from the [[Training Grounds]].

It does not attack. If granted an attack, it shoots a leaf down its lane, making a Straight attack if there is a zombie in range.

Cardboard Umbrella Lead grants the *[[Shielded]]* trait to other plants in a 3x3 area.
