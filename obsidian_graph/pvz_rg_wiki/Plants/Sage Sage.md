## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Sage Sage is a regular plant from the [[Kongfu Mausoleum]] and [[Steam City]], where it is exclusive to [[Greenhouse]] gardens.

It shoots leaves down its lane, making a Straight attack if there is a zombie in range.

At the start of the turn, if there are 2 or more sun producers in the garden, Sage Sage spawns a [[Sunflower Seed]] on a neighbouring tile.
