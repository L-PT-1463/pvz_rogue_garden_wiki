## Origins

Sea-shroom is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it serves as an aquatic counter-part to the [[Puff-shroom]] in the nighttime pool levels.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Sea-shroom is a regular *[[Aquatic]]* plant from the [[Neighbour's House]], exclusive to [[Fall]] gardens.

It shoots spores down its lane at a limited range, making a Straight attack if there is a zombie in range.
