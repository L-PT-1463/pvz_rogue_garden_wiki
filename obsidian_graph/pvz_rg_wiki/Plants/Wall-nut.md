## Origins

Wall-nut is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it serves as the first wall, shielding other plants against incoming zombies.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Wall-nut is a base spawned protector plant, and [[Nate Timely]]'s starting ability.

A Wall-nut appears on a random spot of the lawn, where it stays, blocking the path of zombies until it is vanquished.
