## Origins

Physalux is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2 China, where it serves as the flying counterpart to [[Plantern]] in the Sky City world.

Officially, it is simply called Groundcherry after the plant family it is based on. In the Rogue Garden universe, its name is a portmanteau of physalis, the plant species it is based on, and lux, the Latin word for light.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Physalux is a regular plant from the [[Kongfu Mausoleum]].

<font color="#9bbb59">Speciality</font>: It clears fog in a 3x3 area.

When a zombie with the *[[Invisible]]* trait enters or starts its turn on the 3x3 area around the Plantern, its *Invisible* trait is reduced by 1 stack.

<font color="#f79646">Placement speciality</font>: Physalux is aerial, and can therefore appear and move to any tile.
