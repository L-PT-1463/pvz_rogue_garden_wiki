## Origins

Plantern is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it serves as a support plant that clears up fog in an area of effect.

Its weak attack in PvZ ZPG is based on Plantern's attack from its appearance in Plants vs Zombies Heroes.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Plantern is a regular plant from the [[Neighbour's House]] that attacks in a one tile range in front of itself.

<font color="#9bbb59">Speciality</font>: It clears fog in a 3x3 area.

When a zombie with the *[[Invisible]]* trait enters or starts its turn on the 3x3 area around the Plantern, its *Invisible* trait is reduced by 1 stack.

<font color="#4bacc6">Trait</font>: It has the *[[Stun]]* trait, which means the hit zombie can't move or attack on its next turn.
