## Origins

Tough Beets is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where it increases its stats based on the number of zombies in game. Before its latest balance change, only its health increased..

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Super-Phat Beets is a regular plant from the [[Neon Carnival]].

It generates soundwaves at neighbouring tiles in its lane and column, making a Straight attack if there is a zombie in range.

When a Spawnling is summoned, Super-Phat Beet's health is increased.
