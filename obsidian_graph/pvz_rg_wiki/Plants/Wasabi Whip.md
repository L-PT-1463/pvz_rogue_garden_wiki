## Origins

Wasabi Whip is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2, where it attacks in both directions with a flaming strike.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Wasabi Whip is a potted [[Bonk Choy]] mutation melee plant.

It attacks the closest zombie to itself with a sideways slash, which can hit other zombies.
### Mutations

Wasabi Whip is a mutation of the [[Bonk Choy]], obtained by splicing a Bonk Choy and a [[Cherry Bomb]].
