# Celery Stalker

Celery Stalker is a melee plant that hides until zombies pass by it, attacking them from behind as it protects itself from zombies from the front.

## Origins

Celery Stalker is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2, where it hid underground letting zombies pass by to attack them from behind.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Celery Stalker is a protector plant from [[The Zombosseum]] that can only attack the tile directly behind itself.

<font color="#9bbb59">Speciality</font>: At the start of the turn, if there are no zombies in range, it hides, becoming a regular plant.

<font color="#4bacc6">Trait</font>: When hidden, gains the *[[Grounded]]* trait, and can therefore avoid most attacks.
