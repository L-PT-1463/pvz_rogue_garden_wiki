Atomic Bombegranate is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where, when vanquished, it spawns [[Seedling]]s.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Atomic Bombegranate is a regular plant from [[The Zombosseum]] and [[Steam City]].

It does not attack.

When vanquished, Atomic Bombegranate explodes, making a Straight attack at the 3x3 area around itself. As it does so, it spawns up to 3 [[Seedling]]s in that area, including its own tile.
