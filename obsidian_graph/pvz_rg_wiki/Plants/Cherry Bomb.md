## Origins

Cherry Bomb is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it explodes in a 3x3 area, dealing heavy damage to all zombies caught in the explosion.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Cherry Bomb is a spawned base instant plant and [[Patrice Blazing]]'s starting ability.

When picked from plant selection, a Cherry Bomb is planted on a random spot of the lawn, where it explodes in an area of effect, dealing damage to all zombies caught in its explosion.

When used as part of Patrice's ability, a Cherry Bomb is occasionally thrown at random spots of the lawn.
