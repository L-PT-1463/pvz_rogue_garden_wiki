## Origins

Pea Pod is an official Plants vs Zombies plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2, where multiple can be placed on the same tile, to allow up to five peas to be fired at the zombies at once.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Pea Pod is a potted [[Peashooter]] mutation shooter plant.

It shoots five peas at a time; three in a straight line at the zombie closest to the player and one diagonally to either each.

If another Peashooter-type plant is currently targeting the closest zombie, Pea Pod attacks the second closest instead, and so on.
### Mutations

Pea Pod is a mutation of the [[Peashooter]] obtained in a few ways. It can be obtained by fusing a [[Peashooter]] and a [[Threepeater]], by applying a [[Watering Cane]] to a [[Three Pod]], or by applying [[Fertilizer]] to a Threepeater.
