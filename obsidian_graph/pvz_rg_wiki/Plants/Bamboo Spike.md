## Origins

Bamboo Spike is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2 China, where it attacks the tiles in front of it from below.

Officially, it is known as Bamboo Shoot. Its name was changed in the Rogue Garden universe to avoid confusion with [[Bamboo Shoot]] from Plants vs Zombies Adventure.
