## Origins

Dandelion Weed is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Online, the discontinued China-exclusive game. It attacks zombies by creating explosions in the 3x3 area arond itself.

Officially, it is known as Dandelion, but the name was changed to avoid confusion with the plant also named Dandelion from international Plants vs Zombies 2, renamed here to [[Dandelion Bomber]]. The name Dandelion Weed is in reference to the spawned Dandelion Weeds in the Garden Warfare series, which behave similarly, by exploding themselves.
