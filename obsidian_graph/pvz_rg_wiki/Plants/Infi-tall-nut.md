## Origins

Infi-tall-nut is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the now discontinued, China-exclusive game Plants vs Zombies All Stars. It is the final tier of the [[Wall-nut]] evolutionary line, and is essentially just a [[Tall-nut]] with [[Infi-nut]]'s gimmick.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Infi-tall-nut is a spawned protector plant, and a Level 2 [[Wall-nut]] mutation.

A holographic projector appears at a random spot of the lawn. It periodically generates an Infi-tall-nut, and remains there after the Infi-tall-nut is vanquished.
### Mutations

Infi-tall-nut is a mutation of the [[Wall-nut]], obtained by fusing a Wall-nut and an [[Infi-nut]], or by applying [[Fertilizer]] to the latter.
