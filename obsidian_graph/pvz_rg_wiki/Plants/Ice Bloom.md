## Origins

 Ice Bloom is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2, where it acts as a successor to [[Ice-shroom]], freezing all zombies in the garden and turning the weaker zombies into ice blocks.

When vanquished, Ice Bloom explodes, making a Straight *[[Freeze]]* attack at a 3x3 area around itself.
