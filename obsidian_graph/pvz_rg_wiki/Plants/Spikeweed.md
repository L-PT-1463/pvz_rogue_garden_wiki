## Origins

Spikeweed is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it is ignored by most zombies, allowing it to attack all zombies on its tile at once by piercing them from the ground.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Spikeweed is a standalone spawned melee plant.

When picked from plant selection, a Spikeweed is planted on a random spot of the lawn and stay there until it is vanquished. Spikeweed deals damage to all zombies that walk on top of it.
