## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Chilly Pepper is a regular plant from the [[Neighbour's House]], exclusive to [[Winter]] gardens.

It does not attack.

When vanquished, it makes a Ground *[[Chill]]* attack in its entire lane.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Chilly Pepper is a spawned instant plant, and a Level 2 [[Cherry Bomb]] mutation.

Chilly Pepper appears at a random spot in the lawn, where it explodes in an straight line that stretches into a random direction, dealing damage to all zombies and freezing them.
### Mutations

Chilly Pepper is a mutation of [[Cherry Bomb]], obtained by splicing a [[Cherry Bomb]] and an [[Iceberg Lettuce]].
