## Origins

Sun-shroom is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it serves as the nighttime sun producer, being cheaper than [[Sunflower]] but worse at first.

Sunnier-shroom is also an official plant, making its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes. It is the evolution of Sun-shroom, behaving no differently than Sun-shroom's final stage in the mainline games.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Sun-shroom is a regular plant from the [[Dark Ages]], exclusive to [[Mushroom Grotto]] gardens. It heals the zombie that bit it when hit by a Munch-type attack. After one turn, it grows larger and loses its *Tiny* trait.

<font color="#92d050">Speciality</font>: It has the *Tiny* trait, and can therefor avoid ranged attacks.

At the start of the third turn, it turns into a Sunnier-shroom: A regular plant from the [[Dark Ages]], exclusive to [[Mushroom Grotto]] gardens. It heals the zombie that bit it when bit by a Munch-type attack.
