## Origins

Hypno-shroom is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where upon being consumed by a zombie, it turns it around and makes it attack other zombies before walking off the garden.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Hypno-shroom is a regular plant from the [[Dark Ages]], exclusive to [[Mushroom Grotto]] gardens.

It does not attack.

If it is vanquished by a Munch-type attack, the hit zombie gains 1 stack of *[[Hypnotized]]*.
