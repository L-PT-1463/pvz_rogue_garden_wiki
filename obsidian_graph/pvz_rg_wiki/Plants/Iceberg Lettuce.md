## Origins

Iceberg Lettuce is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2, where it acted as a weak staller and a cheap direct counter to multiple fire-based zombies, being able to freeze a single zombie.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Iceberg Lettuce is a regular *[[Tiny]]* plant from the [[Jurassic Marsh]], where it is [[Frostbite Cave]] gardens, and the [[Neighbour's House]], where it is exclusive to [[Winter]] gardens.

It does not attack. If granted an attack, it lights up, making a Straight *[[Freeze]]* attack at the tile in front of it.

When vanquished, Iceberg Lettuce makes a Straight *[[Freeze]]* attack at the tile in front of it.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Iceberg Lettuce is a standalone spawned staller plant.

An Iceberg Lettce is planted on a random spot of the lawn. When a zombie intercepts it, Iceberg Lettuce explodes, freezing all zombies in a small area of effect.
