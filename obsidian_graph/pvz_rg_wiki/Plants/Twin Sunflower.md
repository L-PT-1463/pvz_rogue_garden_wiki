## Origins

Twin Sunflower is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it is an evolution of the [[Sunflower]] and simply produces twice as much sun.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Twin Sunflower is a potted [[Sunflower]] mutation support plant. It periodically generates rays of sun on two other potted plants at a time which temporarily increase their attack speed.

If a plant is already under the effects of a Sunflower-type plant's buff, Twin Sunflower targets a different plant instead.
### Mutations

Twin Sunflower is a mutation of the [[Sunflower]], obtained by fusing two Sunflowers or by applying [[Fertilizer]] to one.
