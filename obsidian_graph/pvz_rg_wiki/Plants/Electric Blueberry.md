Electric Blueberry is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2, where it strikes a zombie down with lightning after a long cooldown.

It summons large thunder clouds, which target a random zombie on the garden with a Lobbed attack. It has to cool down before attacking again.
