## Origins

Three Pod is a Rogue Garden original inspired by the official plant [[Pea Pod]], the design of which it replicates almost identically, only missing two heads.

It was created as a middleman between [[Repeater]], and [[Gatling Pea]] or Pea Pod in PvZ Lawnmaggedon, shooting three peas in a straight line.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Three Pod is a potted [[Peashooter]] mutation shooter plant.

It shoots three peas at a time in a straight line at the zombie closest to the player.

If another Peashooter-type plant is currently targeting the closest zombie, Three Pod attacks the second closest instead, and so on.
### Mutations

Three Pod is a mutation of the [[Peashooter]], obtained by fusing a Peashooter and a [[Repeater]] or by applying [[Fertilizer]] to the latter.
