Poppin' Poppies is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where it spawns a [[Lil' Buddy]] in its lane and neighbouring lanes.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Poppin' Poppies is a regular plant from [[The Zombosseum]] and [[Steam City]].

It punches zombies in the two tiles in front of itself, making a Straight attack at the front-most zombie in range.

At the start of the turn, if possible, Poppin' Poppies spawns a [[Lil' Buddy]] somewhere in the 3x3 area around it.
