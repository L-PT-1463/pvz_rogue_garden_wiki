## Origins

Volt Snapdragon in the Rogue Garden universe is a combination of two official plants, those being Volt Snapdragon from Plants vs Zombies 2, from which it takes its name and appearance, and Thunder Snapdragon from PvZ 2 China, from which it takes its attack.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Volt Snapdragon is a potted [[Chomper]] mutation ranged plant.

It fires a bolt of electric energy in a straight line at the zombie closest to the player, which explodes upon reaching it, dealing damage in an area of effect.

If there are zombies in its melee area, it instead spits a rapid-fire electric field at a small half-circle area around the zombie closest to it.
### Mutations

Volt Snapdragon is a mutation of [[Chomper]], obtained by splicing an [[E.M.Peach]] and a [[Snapdragon]].
