## Origins

Cardboard Chomper is an official Plants vs Zombie plant from the I, Zombie mini-game in the first Plants vs Zombies game.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Cardboard Chomper is a regular plant from the [[Training Grounds]].

It makes a Straight attack at the zombies in range. It usually has a 1-tile range, which increases by 1 if Cardboard Chomper is behind a protector plant. It has to cool down before attacking again.
