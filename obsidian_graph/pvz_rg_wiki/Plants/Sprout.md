## Origins

Sprout is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it only exists outside of levels as part of the Zen Garden, where it is the first stage of growth of all potted plants.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Sprout is a regular plant from the [[Steam City]], exclusive to [[Greenhouse]] gardens. After a few turns, it turns into a [[Marigold]].

<font color="#92d050">Speciality</font>: It has the *Tiny* trait, and can therefor avoid ranged attacks.
