## Origins

Cardboard Sunflower is an official Plants vs Zombie plant from the I, Zombie mini-game in the first Plants vs Zombies game.
## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Cardboard Sunflower is a regular plant from the [[Training Grounds]].

It does not attack.

If it is vanquished by a Munch attack, it heals the zombie that bit it.
