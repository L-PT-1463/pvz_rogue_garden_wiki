## Origins

Chili Bean is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2, where after being eaten it kills the zombie and stuns the rest of the lane.

Its attack in PvZ ZPG is a reference to its [[Plant Food]] power in Plants vs Zombies 2.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Chili Bean is a regular plant.

It does not attack. If granted an attack, it farts, making a Straight *[[Pierce]]* attack at the 3x3 area around itself.

If vanquished by a Munch attack, it makes a Straight *[[Pierce]]* and *[[Strike-through]]*, *[[Stun]]* attack down its lane.
