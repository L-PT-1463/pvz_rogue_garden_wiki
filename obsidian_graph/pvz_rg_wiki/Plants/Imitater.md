## Origins

Imitater is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it acts as a copy of another plant in seed selection, allowing the player to bypass seed recharge time.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Imitater is a regular plant from the [[Neon Carnival]] that copies a neighbouring plant.

<font color="#9bbb59">Speciality</font>: At the start of the turn, it choses a nearby plant to copy.

It prioritises the plant in front, then behind, then on the neighbouring lane above, then bellow, then the diagonals from left to right, top to bottom. Imitater can also copy plants with the *[[Vine]]* and *[[Pot]]* traits, though it prioritises plants without those traits.

While it copies a plant, it behaves exactly as the copied plant would. If the plant is vanquised, at the start of its next turn it will copy a different neighbour, or revert to its regular form if there are no more neighbours.
