## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Venus Flytrap is a regular plant the [[Jurassic Marsh]] and [[Steam City]], where it is exclusive to [[Greenhouse]] gardens.

It bites at the two tiles in front of itself, making a Straight attack at the front-most zombie in range. This heals the plant in front of Venus Flytrap for the damage dealt.
