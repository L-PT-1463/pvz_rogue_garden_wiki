## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Snowdrop is a regular plant from the [[Neighbour's House]] and [[Old Wild Egypt]].

It shoots icy water droplets, making a Lobbed attack at the front-most zombie in its lane.

When any zombie is affected by a *[[Chill]]* or *[[Freeze]]* attack, Snowdrop gains a slight stat boost.
