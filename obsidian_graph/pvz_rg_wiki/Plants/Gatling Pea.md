## Origins

Gatling Pea is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it is an evolution of the [[Repeater]] that shoots four peas at a time at the zombies in its lane.

Its appearance in the Rogue Garden universe takes inspiration from Plants vs Zombies 2's Mega Gatling Pea, which also has leaves pressed under the helmet to resemble hair.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Gatling Pea is a regular plant from the [[Neighbour's House]].

It shoots rapid fire *[[Flammable]]* peas down its lane, making a *[[Spread]] (4)* Straight attack if there is a zombie in range.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Gatling Pea is a potted shooter plant, and a Level 3 [[Peashooter]] mutation.

It shoots four peas at a time in a straight line at the zombie closest to the player.

If another Peashooter-type plant is currently targeting the closest zombie, Gatling Pea attacks the second closest instead, and so on.
### Mutations

Gatling Pea is a mutation of the [[Peashooter]], obtained by fusing a Peashooter and a [[Three Pod]], or by applying [[Fertilizer]] to the latter.
