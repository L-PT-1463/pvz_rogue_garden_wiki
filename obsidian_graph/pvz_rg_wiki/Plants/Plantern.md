## Origins

Plantern is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it serves as a support plant that clears up fog in an area of effect.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Plantern is a regular plant from the [[Neighbour's House]] that attacks in a one tile range in front of itself. It also removes fog in a 3x3 area.

<font color="#9bbb59">Speciality</font>: It has the *Stun* trait, which means the hit zombie can't move or attack on its next turn.
<font color="#9bbb59">Speciality</font>: When a zombie with the *Invisible* trait enters or starts its turn on the 3x3 area around the Plantern, its *Invisible* trait is reduced by 1 stack.
