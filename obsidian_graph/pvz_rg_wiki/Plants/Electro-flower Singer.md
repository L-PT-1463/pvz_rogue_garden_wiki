## Origins

Electro-flower Singer is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the fourth Chinese beta of Plants vs Zombies 3, where it acts as a sun producer that boosts the attack of plants around it, and becomes more effective while in an electric grid.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Electro-flower Singer is a regular plant from the [[Far Future]].

It does not attack. If granted an attack, it sings to release a soundwave down its lane, making a Straight attack if there is a zombie in range.

Electro-flower Singer boosts the damage of other plants in a 3x3 area around itself.

When hit by a Munch attack, it heals the zombie that bit it.
