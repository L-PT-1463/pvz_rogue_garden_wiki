## Origins

Ace-sparagus is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2 China, where it shoots rapid fire shots at the tiles in front of itself in its lane and neighbouring lanes. It has to recharge after attacking.

Officially, it is known as Asparagus (or Asparagus Fighter, literal translation). Its name was changed for clarity, as to make it more directly related to the plant itself and avoid confusion with other asparagus-based plants.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Ace-paragus is a regular plant from the [[Steam City]], exclusive to [[Sky Battle]] gardens. 

<font color="#9bbb59">Speciality</font>: It can target any zombie in the 3x3 area in front of it, and can spread its three shots per attack between various zombies.

<font color="#4bacc6">Trait</font>: It has the *[[Spread]] (3)* trait, so fires three times per attack.
