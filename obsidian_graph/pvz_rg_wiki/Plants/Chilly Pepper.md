## Origins

Chilly Pepper is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Adventures, where it acts as an icy counter-part to the [[Jalapeño]].

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Chilly Pepper is a regular plant from the [[Neighbour's House]], exclusive to [[Winter]] gardens.

<font color="#8064a2">When vanquished</font>: It explodes, attacking all zombies in its lane, but dealing no damage.

<font color="#4bacc6">Trait</font>: It has the *Freeze* trait, which means the hit zombie can't move or attack on its next turn.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Jalapeño is a spawned [[Cherry Bomb]] mutation instant plant.

When used as part of Patrice's ability, a Jalapeño is occasionally thrown at random spots of the lawn, where it explodes in an straight line that stretches into a random direction, dealing damage to all zombies caught in its fire.
### Mutations

Jalapeño is a mutation of [[Cherry Bomb]], obtained by fusing two Cherry Bombs.
