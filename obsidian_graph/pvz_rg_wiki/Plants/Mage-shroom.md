## Origins

Mage-shroom is based on two official plants from Plants vs Zombies 2 China, those being Cryo-shroom and Pyro-shroom. In their origin game, they are exact copies of one another, firing elemental projectiles that power up over time in three stages. The only difference between them is Cryo-shroom shoots ice and Pyro-shroom shoots fire.

They were reworked into a singular plant for PvZ ZPG as it seemed more interesting to do so.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Mage-shroom is a regular plant from the [[Dark Ages]].

On its turn, it either shoots a ray of frost down its lane, making a Straight *[[Chill]]* attack if there's a zombie in range, or shoots down a bolt of fire, making a Lobbed *[[Fire]]* attack at the front-most zombie.
