## Origins

Flower Pot is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it acted as the only way to plant other plants on the roof level's tiled floor.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Flower Pot is a simple pass-through plant.

<font color="#9bbb59">Speciality</font>: It has the *Pot* trait, allowing plants to appear on its tile. Because of this, it cannot be targeted by ranged attacks and the plant on top must be vanquished before it.
