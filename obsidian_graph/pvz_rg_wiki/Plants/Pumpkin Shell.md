## Origins

Pumpkin Shell is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it serves as a wall plant that can be planted on the same tile as another plant.

Its name is taken from its iteration in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, in order to avoid confusion with other pumpkin-based plants.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Pumpkin Shell is a protector plant from the [[Dark Ages]].
