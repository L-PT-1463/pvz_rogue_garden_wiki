## Origins

Rotten Red is a Plants vs Zombies designed by a boy named Miles for a collaboration between Pop Cap and the make-A-Wish Foundation. It was added to Plants vs Zombies 2 China, though it was quickly removed as the developers lacked the necessary permissions to use the plant.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Rotten Red is a regular plant from the [[Kongfu Mausoleum]] and [[Old Wild Egypt]] that fires down its lane.

<font color="#4bacc6">Trait</font>: If it hit a zombie on the previous turn, its attack has the *[[Knockback]]* trait, meaning it has a chance to push the hit zombie back one tile.
