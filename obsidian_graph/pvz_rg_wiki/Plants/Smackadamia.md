## Origins

Smackadamia is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where it increases the health of all nuts when it is planted.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Smackadamia is a regular plant from the [[Pirate's Beach]] that attacks the tile directly in front of itself.

<font color="#9bbb59">Speciality</font>: At the start of the first turn, Smackadamia increases the health of all [[Nut Tribe]] plants.

<font color="#f79646">Placement speciality</font>: Smackadamia is aerial, and can therefore appear and move to any tile.

<font color="#4bacc6">Trait</font>: Its attack has the *[[Knockback]]* trait, meaning it has a chance to push the hit zombie back one tile.
