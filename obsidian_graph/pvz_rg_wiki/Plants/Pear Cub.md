## Origins

Pear Cub is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where it is a basic low-stat amphibious plant that spawns a [[Grizzly Pear]] when vanquished.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Pear Cub is a regular plant from the [[Jurassic Marsh]].

It bites at the tile directly in front of itself, making a Straight attack if there is a zombie in range.

When vanquished, Pear Cub spawns a [[Grizzly Pear]] in its tile.
