## Origins

Fire Pea is based on the official Plants vs Zombie plant Flaming Pea, most commonly known by its later appearances as Fire Peashooter, that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Adventures, where it serves as a fire variant of the [[Peashooter]].

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Fire Pea is a potted [[Peashooter]] mutation shooter plant.

It shoots one burning pea at a time in a straight line at the zombie closest to the player, that deals splash damage in a small area.

If another Peashooter-type plant is currently targeting the closest zombie, Fire Pea attacks the second closest instead, and so on.
### Mutations

Fire Pea is a mutation of the [[Peashooter]], obtained by splicing a Peashooter and a [[Cherry Bomb]].
