## Origins

Cardboard Garlic is an official Plants vs Zombie plant from the I, Zombie mini-game in the first Plants vs Zombies game.

Its attack in PvZ ZPG is a reference to [[Garlic]]'s [[Plant Food]] power in Plants vs Zombies 2.
## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Cardboard Garlic is a protector plant from the [[Training Grounds]].

It does not attack. If granted an attack, it breaths garlic breath down its lane, making a Straight *[[Pierce]]* and *[[Strike-through]]* attack if there is a zombie in range. Zombies caught in its breath move to an adjacent lane if the space allows it.

When hit by a Munch attack, it moves that zombie to an adjacent lane, if the space allows it.
