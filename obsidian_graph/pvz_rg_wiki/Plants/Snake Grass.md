Snake Grass is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where at the start of each turn, it creates a duplicate of itself.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Snake Grass is a regular *[[Amphibious]]* plant from [[The Zombosseum]], the [[Lost City]] and the [[Neon Carnival]], where it is exclusive to [[Bog of Enlightenment]] gardens.

It attacks the two tiles directly in front of itself, making a Straight attack at the front-most zombie in range. 

At the start of the turn, Snake Grass creates another Snake Grass in the tile in front of itself when possible.
