## Origins

Moonbean is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where it shuffles Magical Beanstalks into the player's deck after it attacks.

Moonbean was completely reworked for the Rogue Garden universe, keeping her appearance and attack, but serving a vastly different purpose as a [[Conceal-mint Family]] plant. This usage was only given to it during the conception of PvZ Lawnmaggedon, wherein Moonbeam was repurposed into the Conceal-mint instant use plant, serving a similar role to [[Iceberg Lettuce]] or [[E.M.Peach]] in that game.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Moonbean is a regular plant from the [[Dark Ages]], [[Neon Carnival]], [[ZCorp IST Headquarters]], and [[Far Future]], where it is exclusive to [[Moon Base Z]] gardens.

It fires a moonbeam at the tile directly in front of itself, making a Straight [[Pierce]] attack if there is a zombie in range. When it attacks, it shadow-powers other tiles in its lane for the remainder of that turn.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Moonbean is a standalone spawned instant plant.

A Moonbean appears on a random spot of the lawn, where it blasts a moonbeam down on the zombies, inflicting damage in an area of effect.

If a [[Murkadamia Nut]] is caught in the moonbeam, if becomes temporarily powered, even if the player has no [[Moonflower]].
