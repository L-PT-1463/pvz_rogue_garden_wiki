## Origins

Spineapple is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies Heroes, where it increases the attack damage of all plants with zero attack.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Spineapple is a regular plant from the [[Pirate's Beach]] and [[The Zombosseum]].

It shoots leaves down its lane, making a Straight attack if there is a zombie in range.

Spineapple grants every plant in a 4x3 area, 2 tiles forwards and 1 tile backwards, the ability to attack.
