## Origins

Black Orchid is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in Plants vs Zombies 2 China, where it fires gravity balls at the zombies, damaging them and sucking them into its lane.

Its name in the Rogue Garden universe, Black Orchid, is a portmanteau of black hole and orchid. Officially, it is known as Orchid Mage (or Clamshell Orchid Mage, literal translation).

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Black Orchid is a regular plant from [[ZCorp IST Headquarters]] that fires gravitational spheres at the zombies.

<font color="#9bbb59">Speciality</font>: Its attack is unaffected by [[Black Hole]] gardens. When possible, causes zombies in the 3x3 area around the hit zombie to move to its lane.
