## Origins

Cardboard Potato Mine is an official Plants vs Zombie plant from the I, Zombie mini-game in the first Plants vs Zombies game.
## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Cardboard Potato Mine is a pass-through *[[Tiny]]* plant from the [[Training Grounds]].

If there is a zombie on its tile, it makes a Ground attack, vanquishing itself as it does so.
