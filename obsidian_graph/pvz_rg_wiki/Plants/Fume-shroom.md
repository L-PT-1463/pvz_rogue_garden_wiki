## Origins

Fume-shroom is an official Plants vs Zombie plant that made its debut in the first Plants vs Zombies game, where it can hit multiple zombies with its attack, and acts as a counter against the [[Screendoor Zombie]], whose shield the Fume-shroom can ignore.

## In PvZ ZPG - Zombie RPG

Fume-shroom is a regular plant from the [[Dark Ages]], exclusive to [[Mushroom Grotto]] gardens.

It fires fumes at a limited range, making a Straight *[[Pierce]]* and *[[Strike-through]]* attack if there is a zombie in range.

## In PvZ: Lawnmaggedon

Fume-shroom is a potted shooter plant, and a Level 1 [[Puff-shroom]] mutation.

It fires a cloud of spores in a straight line at the zombie closest to the player. All zombies caught in the cloud of spores are hit by the attack.

If another Puff-shroom-type plant is currently targeting the closest zombie, Fume-shroom attacks the second closest instead, and so on.
### Mutations

Fume-shroom is a mutation of the [[Puff-shroom]], obtained by fusing two Puff-shrooms, or by applying [[Fertilizer]] to one.
